10 · Touch button

  • Moaan's own options for the button, which only its (now disabled) launcher could set. Same choices, same meaning.
  • Shown with your current setup: tap = Home, hold = Full refresh.
  • Changes apply on the next touch; nothing restarts.
  • Lives under Whole device on the home screen, not in the quick panel.
  • Uses the permission InkPalm+ already has for turning its panel back on; without it, the section explains how to grant it.
  • Home goes to your current home app (inkOS), not Moaan's launcher.
