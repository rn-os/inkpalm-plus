21 · Frozen vs Live

Frozen: the screen shows the time you pressed the power button. Nothing runs while it sleeps, so after 3 hours it is 3 hours old, and that is normal.

Live: the phone wakes every 15 / 30 / 60 min, redraws, and sleeps again. The face carries a small Updated 10:15 stamp, so a stale time is obvious (it can only be as old as the interval, or older if the phone is off or the battery is flat).

Low battery (idea): below 10% the face swaps to a black box, and Live updates stop to save what is left.
