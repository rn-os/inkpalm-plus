21 · Sleep screen settings

  • Face: tap a thumbnail. The tick marks the one in use. "Default" is Moaan's own picture.
  • Update: Frozen is free. The others wake the phone to redraw (about 3 s, one flash).
  • Battery cost is an estimate: ~0.33 mAh per update (3 s at ~400 mA) on a ~2500 mAh cell gives 1.3% / 0.7% / 0.3% a day for 15 / 30 / 60 min. Real numbers need a device measurement; the idle alarm wake adds a little.
  • Show seconds: fixed at Never, shown so the answer is visible.
  • Frontlight: the update forces it off; it is not a setting you can change here.
  • Agenda would need calendar access; the picker asks the first time it is chosen.
