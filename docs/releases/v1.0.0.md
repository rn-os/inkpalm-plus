**InkPalm+ 1.0.0**: the first stable release. A side panel of recent apps for the Moaan InkPalm Plus: swipe up or down along the screen edge in any app.

**What's in it**
- **Recent apps,** newest first. The app you came from is marked with a ← badge, one tap away.
- **Pages you swipe** instead of scrolling, so nothing smears on e-ink. Opens in about 30 ms. Works in landscape.
- **✕ on each app** to close it, and **Clear all**. Hold an app to remove it from the list, open its e-ink settings or its app info.
- **E-ink panel for the app in front:** a preset (Reading, Browsing, Comics & photos, Video), size (DPI, applied live), contrast and rotation. The InkPalm has no rotation sensor, so this is how you turn the screen.
- **Font** for every app, live. **Touch button:** pick what tap and hold do.
- **Keeps the panel on:** survives Moaan's Clear Memory (with Shizuku or a one-time adb command) and turns itself back on if it's switched off by mistake.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your InkPalm and open it, or add `https://github.com/rn-os/inkpalm-plus` in Obtainium. Then open InkPalm+, tap **Side panel** and turn on the accessibility service.
No internet permission.

Tested on the InkPalm Plus (firmware MAS_EPD305_L81B804_T44_V09, Android 11). Feedback and bug reports: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/rn-os/inkpalm-plus/issues).
