**InkPalm+ 1.1.0**: KompaktOS support. On the [KompaktOS port](https://github.com/rn-os/kompaktos-inkpalm-plus) for the InkPalm Plus, InkPalm+ now does everything InkControl did, so InkControl isn't needed any more (uninstall it, so the two don't both drive the screen and light). On the stock firmware nothing changes.

**New on KompaktOS**
- **Screen & light panel:** swipe up or down along the other edge (the left one, unless recent apps are there). Six screen modes as big buttons (Fast, Soft, Dither, Text, Quality, Auto), for the app you're in or everywhere, with a line saying where the mode in use comes from. **Full refresh**, **Contrast**, and the front light (Brightness, Warmth, **Auto**).
- The front light moved here from the recent-apps panel, so recent apps get their rows back. It goes off with the screen and comes back with it, and follows Android's brightness or the light sensor.
- **Edge Back:** swipe in from either edge to go back (KompaktOS has no navigation bar). On by default; a switch under Side panel.
- **Touch button:** a short touch is Home; holding it is a full refresh, as on the stock firmware.
- **Screen & light settings:** your own sleep screen, the power-off screen (Moaan's panda in English, or your own picture), and auto power-off after 4 to 24 hours asleep on battery.
- Quick Settings tiles for the screen modes, Full refresh and the front light.

**Install:** download the `.apk` below on your InkPalm and open it, or add `https://github.com/rn-os/inkpalm-plus` in Obtainium. It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your settings. New permissions: exact alarms (auto power-off) and asking to uninstall an app (the Uninstall InkControl button).
No internet permission. Now under the Apache 2.0 license.

Tested on the InkPalm Plus with KompaktOS 1.2 (Android 16) and the stock firmware's side panel. The English panda power-off screen needs the latest KompaktOS port image. Feedback and bug reports: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/rn-os/inkpalm-plus/issues).
